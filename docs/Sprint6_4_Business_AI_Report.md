# Sprint 6.4 — Business Communication Agent Report

## 1. Engine Overview
`BusinessAgentEngine.kt` adds enterprise communication intelligence to automatically detect customer inquiries, calculate customer attention scores, and surface sales opportunities.

## 2. Business Metrics & Dashboard Enhancements
- **Customer Attention Score**: Aggregated metric (0–100) prioritizing high-value customer interactions.
- **Pending Replies**: Unanswered incoming customer inquiries.
- **Sales Opportunities**: Auto-detected price requests, catalog inquiries, and orders.
- **Suggested Follow-Ups**: Contextual next-steps recommended for enterprise team members.
