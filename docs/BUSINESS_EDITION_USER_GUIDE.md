# Global SMS — Business Edition User Guide

## Overview
Global SMS Business Edition empowers enterprises with offline AI intelligence, role-based access control, automated customer workflows, and multi-device synchronization.

## Key Features & How-To

### 1. Organization & Department Management
- Navigate to **Enterprise Dashboard**.
- View company details, department structures, and employee status.

### 2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Navigate to **Permission Management Screen**.
- Switch active user role (OWNER, ADMIN, MANAGER, EMPLOYEE, VIEW_ONLY) to test permissions in real-time.

### 3. Business Messaging & Campaigns
- Open **Business Messaging Screen**.
- Choose a template (Invoice, OTP, Meeting Reminder) and input variables (`{name}`, `{invoice}`, `{date}`, `{code}`).
- Enable **Manager Approval Workflow** if required.

### 4. Customer 360 CRM
- Open **Customer 360 Screen**.
- Track customer lifecycle stages (Lead, Customer, VIP, Inactive).
- View full timeline of interactions including SMS, call logs, tasks, notes, and AI insights.

### 5. Multi-Device Companion Sync
- Automatic background synchronization across phones, tablets, foldables, and desktop/wear companions using AES-256 E2EE.
