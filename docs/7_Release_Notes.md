## What's New in Version 1.0.0

- **3 Modern UI Systems:** Classic SMS, Smart AI Dashboard, and Enterprise CRM views.
- **Dual SIM Engine:** Explicit SIM 1 / SIM 2 selection with individual delivery status tracking.
- **Private Vault:** Biometric & PIN protected vault with `FLAG_SECURE` screenshot prevention.
- **Persian & RTL Localization:** Full Persian text normalization, 7 emoji categories, and Vazirmatn/Samim/Yekan Persian fonts.
- **AES-256 Encrypted Backups:** Local and cloud backup export/import with passphrase protection.
- **Google Play Compliant:** 100% policy-compliant Default SMS Handler implementation.
